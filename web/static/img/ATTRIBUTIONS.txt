Medee fork — generic corporate status icons (happy.webp, pensive.webp, reject.webp).

These images were drawn in code with Pillow (flat geometric shapes, no
third-party artwork) for the Medee corporate fork. They replace the upstream
mascot artwork and are available under the same MIT license as this
repository (see LICENSE).

Upstream mascot artwork by CELPHASE is not shipped in this fork.
